- qwreey-conventions:code-reuse: Steps for a definition that must live in two places. Marks a copy that cannot be imported with @copy-of or @start-replace region markers, syncs regions with sync-copies, compares a copy with its source in a test, and measures a library against a hand-written parser or algorithm. Use it when copying a definition into another package, language or repo, when adding region markers or acting on a reuse-check notice, and before hand-writing a parser or algorithm a well-known library provides. 사본 표시, 영역 복사, 라이브러리 대신 직접 구현.
